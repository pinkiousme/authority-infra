The mechanism

The Intent-to-Meeting Intelligence System™

Five stages, run for you and reviewed with you every week.

  1. 1

    Competitor intelligence

    We map your competitors and their positioning, study their LinkedIn posts, and identify the relevant people engaging with them.

  2. 2

    Buying-signal detection

    We look for events that may point to a need for your service: sales or marketing hires, new executives, funding, expansion, launches, new markets. Each signal is verified before we use it.

  3. 3

    High-intent keyword research

    We research the service, industry and pain-point language your buyers use, and monitor the LinkedIn conversations where it appears.

  4. 4

    Prospect intelligence

    For each prospect we review the company, role, website and public activity, note likely challenges, score the lead and record the source and research date.

  5. 5

    Personalised conversion

    Connection requests, direct messages and follow-ups written for each prospect, then qualification and optimisation based on what the replies tell us.

Every prospect arrives with receipts: who they are, what changed, where we saw it, and the angle most likely to earn a reply.

Questions

Straight answers

Is this just another lead list?

No. A list tells you who exists. We tell you who is showing signs they may need you, why, and what to say. Every prospect comes with a documented relevance reason, a supporting signal, the source and the research date, plus a personalised outreach angle.

Does a buying signal mean the prospect wants to buy?

No, and we never claim it does. A hiring push, a funding round or a new executive suggests there may be a need. We verify each signal is relevant to your offer before we use it. The conversation then tells us whether the need is real.

Which execution path is right for me?

If you want the whole outbound process off your plate, choose the managed path: we run the outreach, follow-ups, qualification and booking coordination. If you prefer to keep full control of your LinkedIn account and send messages yourself, choose the research-led path: we deliver researched prospects with ready-to-send personalised messages. Both use the same research foundation. We will recommend one on the strategy call.

Is the 5 to 15 opportunities a month a promise?

It is a target, not a promise. It depends on your offer, your market, the path you choose, how quickly you approve messaging and the qualification criteria we agree in writing.

How does the performance commitment work?

If the agreed minimum target is not reached in the final 30 days of the initial 90-day engagement, despite you fulfilling your agreed responsibilities, we continue management or research delivery for up to 30 additional days without an additional management fee. Qualified-opportunity definitions, your obligations and exclusions are set out in writing.

Do you guarantee revenue or closed deals?

No. We do not guarantee revenue or closed deals, and we cannot promise LinkedIn account safety. We create qualified conversations. Closing them is your job.

How much does it cost?

There is no public price list, because the investment depends on how much research depth your market needs, how many prospects you want, how complex your market is and how much of the execution you want us to run. After a short strategy call you receive a clear proposal that shows exactly what is included, before you commit to anything. Any third-party costs are specified in writing in advance.

Will the outreach sound robotic, and what about my LinkedIn account?

Every message is written from the prospect's business, role and context, and you approve the messaging before anything goes out. LinkedIn restricts some kinds of automation and no provider can remove that risk completely, so we use controlled volumes and review early performance before scaling. Any account arrangement is agreed with you in writing and requires your authorisation.

What do I need to do?

Share your offer and ideal customer, approve the messaging, give feedback on lead quality and take the calls that arrive. On the research-led path you also send the prepared messages and book your own calls.

Who is this not for?

Agencies that expect lead generation to make up for a weak offer, a shaky sales process or limited capacity to serve new clients. Also anyone who wants a contact list, guaranteed revenue or ban-proof promises. If we are not a fit, I will tell you on the strategy call.
