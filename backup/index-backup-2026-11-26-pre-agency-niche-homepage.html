Questions

Straight answers

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A qualified meeting is one where the prospect matches the agreed ICP, is a relevant decision-maker or buying stakeholder, has shown interest in a relevant business conversation, and has accepted a calendar invitation. We write this definition into the service agreement.

Do you guarantee sales or revenue?

No. We do not guarantee closed revenue, and a booked meeting is not the same as a completed sales opportunity. The system is designed to create more qualified conversations. Closing them is your job.

How does the guarantee work?

If you complete the agreed onboarding, give timely approvals and take part as agreed, but we miss the agreed qualified-meeting target in the measurement period, we keep optimising and delivering at no additional service fee for an agreed extension period. The target, period and your responsibilities are documented in the service agreement.

Is the 10 to 30 calls a month a promise?

It is a target, not a promise. The system is designed to aim for 10 to 30 qualified sales conversations a month once the campaign is optimised. Results depend on your offer, your market, how fast you approve things and how the campaign performs.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is tailored to each business, because every client's market, goals and requirements are different. After the strategy call you receive a clear, personalised proposal that shows exactly what is included and the investment, before you commit to anything. Any third-party costs are discussed and agreed with you in advance.

Will the outreach sound robotic or put my LinkedIn account at risk?

Every message is personalised from the prospect's business, role and context, and you approve the messaging before launch. Replies and follow-ups are handled with human oversight. LinkedIn restricts some kinds of automation and no provider can remove that risk completely, so we start with controlled outreach volumes and review early performance before scaling.

What do I need to do?

Complete onboarding, approve the messaging, and take the qualified calls that arrive. You focus on sales conversations while the outbound system is managed for you.

What happens after the 8 weeks?

In Week 8 you get a handover session, the documented outbound process (SOPs), your performance dashboard and a 90-day growth roadmap. You will know which campaigns and segments work best and what to do next.

Who is this not for?

Founders with no paying customers yet, low-value or consumer products, no clear target market, or no capacity for more sales calls. If we are not a fit, I will tell you on the strategy call.
