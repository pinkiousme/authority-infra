The Diagnosis

You have the expertise. You still can’t see which companies need a CFO before your competitors do.

You built your practice on real financial and operational expertise. That work is real, and it still matters.

Most fractional CFO firms respond to a quiet pipeline the same way: post more, work the same referral relationships harder, join another peer community. That builds relationships. It does not tell you which company down the street just raised a round, is scaling headcount with no finance leader in sight, or just went through an ownership change.

The problem is not that you are not working your network. The problem is that the signals are already public, buried across job boards, funding databases, and press releases, and by the time a referral finally lands, another fractional CFO firm has already had the conversation.

Fractional CFO engagements are considered, high-value relationships, not impulse purchases. A quiet pipeline is not a vague growth concern. It is a direct, calculable gap in the practice you have already built the expertise to serve, and the only way to close it is to see the signal, verified, before anyone else does.

Common Questions

What you are probably already thinking

What does it cost?

Shared once a discovery call has confirmed genuine fit, and once you have seen your own free report. Not before.

Every practice has a different ICP and volume need, so there is no published rate card. What you can verify before any pricing conversation: the actual research methodology, the evidence standard, and a real report built from your own market. Book a 30-minute walkthrough to see it first.

Is this a subscription?

Yes. The CFO Opportunity Engine is a monthly service: a new, verified report delivered on a set cadence. There is no long-term contract, and the specific terms and cadence are confirmed on the call once you have seen your free report.

What is a CFO opportunity, and how is it different from a lead?

A lead is a name. An opportunity is a name plus a reason: a verified signal, funding, headcount growth, finance hiring, an ownership change, or a similar trigger, evidence you can check, the right decision-maker, and a personalised reason to reach out now. If any of that is missing, it does not count as an opportunity here.

What signals do you track?

Funding rounds, rapid headcount growth, finance hiring with no CFO in place, ownership changes, ERP or accounting-system transitions, and leadership changes, calibrated to your specific specialisation. Not every signal applies to every practice; your actual ICP determines which ones matter.

How do you verify a signal?

Every material claim is checked against public sources before it reaches you: company websites, official announcements, filings, and credible industry publications carry more weight than a directory listing or a social post. If the evidence is not strong enough, the opportunity does not ship, and we say “not verified” rather than fill the gap with a guess.

Can this be customised to my practice?

Yes. Calibrated to your sector, company stage, geography, and specific specialisation from the start, whether you specialise narrowly or work as a generalist.

Do you contact prospects on my behalf?

No. You receive the research, the decision-maker, and a suggested opening message. You make contact yourself, from your own account, in your own voice. No credentials change hands, and no automation touches your LinkedIn account.

Is there a guarantee?

There is no guarantee on outcomes. Anyone who promises you a specific number of new clients from a research service is selling you something they cannot deliver. What you get instead: a free, real report before you ever discuss pricing, so you can judge the evidence yourself before committing to anything.

What does “direct operator access” actually mean?

You email me, Saurabh, directly at saurabh@pipelind.com. Not a project manager. Not an account team. The same person who builds every report responds within 24 hours, Monday through Friday.

Why fractional CFO firms specifically?

Referral dependency is the single most-repeated complaint fractional CFOs make about their own practice. Fractional CFO engagements are considered, high-value relationships, so a quiet pipeline is a direct, calculable income gap, not a vague growth concern. This is also a genuinely LinkedIn-active professional audience, which is what makes a signal-based, evidence-first approach workable rather than theoretical.

I already use a lead-gen or prospecting tool. Why do I need this?

Those tools give you raw access, more names, more data, and leave the verification, prioritisation, and personalisation entirely to you. Pipelind does that work before it reaches you: a small number of opportunities, already checked, ranked, and matched to a specific reason to reach out, not another database to sort through yourself.
